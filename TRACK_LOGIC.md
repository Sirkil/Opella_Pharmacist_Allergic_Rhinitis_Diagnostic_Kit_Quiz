# Seven-question quiz and track rules

The quiz now asks about nasal discharge, itching, sneezing, congestion with mild malaise, fever with facial pain, duration, and AQI with symptom worsening. The combined choices preserve the signs used by the three tracks without making the user answer 12 separate questions.

| Track shown in result hero | Defining answers |
| --- | --- |
| **المسار أ — حساسية تتفاقم بسبب التلوّث** | Watery clear discharge, itching, repeated sneezing bouts, **no congestion or mild malaise**, no fever or facial pain, and AQI 101–150 or 151+ **with** worsening symptoms. The deck does not set a duration for this path; a duration over 14 days triggers review. |
| **المسار ب — نزلة برد / عدوى فيروسية** | Clear to slightly cloudy discharge, sneezing **without repeated bouts**, both congestion and mild malaise, **no itching, fever, or facial pain**, symptoms for 7–10 days, and no reported link between pollution and worsening symptoms. |
| **المسار ج — عدوى (التهاب الجيوب البكتيري) · إحالة** | Thick yellow or green discharge, both fever and facial pain, symptoms for more than 14 days, and **no itching, sneezing, congestion, or mild malaise**. Air answers do not override this warning pattern. This is a track name from the supplied deck, not an automated diagnosis. |

Every question must be answered before the result appears. An off-path symptom prevents an exact match and gives **مسار غير محدد**. A warning sign with Track A or B also gives **مسار غير محدد** and asks for pharmacist review. Track C requires all four warning signs and the negative answers listed above.

The supplied deck does not explicitly specify every negative answer. The Yes/No exclusions above implement the requested path-specific matching. A result with no completed track still shows warning signs in its pharmacist guidance when present.
